2. Vehicle Diagnostic Data

K-Gauge connects to a compatible OBD diagnostic adapter via Bluetooth and reads diagnostic data made available by the vehicle's electronic systems.

Depending on the selected vehicle profile, this may include engine operating parameters, temperatures, pressures, DPF-related information, electronic control unit (ECU) data, and other diagnostic parameters.

This data is processed locally on the user's device for the purpose of providing K-Gauge functionality. It is not automatically transmitted to or stored by K-Lab.

3. Diagnostic Reports and Communication

K-Gauge allows users to manually export diagnostic reports.

Diagnostic data may be shared with K-Lab only at the user's initiative, for example by manually exporting and sending a diagnostic report.

If a user sends a diagnostic report or contacts K-Lab by email, the information provided, including the user's email address and any technical information contained in the report, may be processed for the purpose of analyzing the issue, diagnosing technical problems, and providing support.

Email communication with K-Lab is handled using Apple iCloud services. Such processing is subject to Apple's applicable privacy and data-protection practices and may involve international processing or transfers of data in accordance with applicable law.

4. Purchases

Purchases of the application, vehicle profiles, or other digital features are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

K-Gauge uses transaction and entitlement information provided by StoreKit to determine whether the user has access to a purchased feature or vehicle profile.

Payments are processed by Apple. K-Lab does not directly process payment card details or Apple ID login credentials.

5. Crash Reports

Apple may provide K-Lab with crash reports and related technical diagnostic information concerning K-Gauge, in accordance with Apple's developer services and the user's applicable privacy and analytics settings.

This information may be used to diagnose software issues and improve the stability and operation of K-Gauge.
